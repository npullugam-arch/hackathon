How the estimate works

Daily income is assumed to continue for every day of the duration. The average is the midpoint of your range, not a probability-weighted forecast.

Purchase cost is subtracted once. Fees, taxes, reinvestment and resale value are excluded. Totals use unrounded values; displayed amounts are rounded to two decimals.
