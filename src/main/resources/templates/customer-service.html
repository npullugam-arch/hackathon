How does Recharge work?

Recharge uses Razorpay Checkout. After a captured payment is confirmed by the server, the amount is added to your separate Recharge Balance in INR. Recharge money is used for product purchases and is not withdrawable as Winning Cash.

How do products or memberships work?

Choose an active product, review its price, daily income and total claims, then buy it using Recharge Balance. The product terms are saved at purchase time and its earning lifecycle starts from the configured claim schedule.

How is daily income generated?

Daily income is configured for each product by the administrator. It is released through eligible daily claims and credited to Winning Cash, not automatically to Recharge Balance.

How does daily claiming work?

Open My Products when a claim is available and select Claim. The server checks ownership, product status, timezone, claim date and duplicate protection before crediting the exact configured amount.

What if I do not claim a daily income?

Missed claims expire at the next daily boundary. They are not automatically paid later, and the missed amount is shown in your product lifecycle details.

How long does a product last?

The product runs for its configured number of days. After the final eligible day it becomes Completed, the claim action stops, and no income beyond daily income multiplied by the duration can be claimed.

What is Winning Cash?

Winning Cash is the separate balance created by eligible earnings such as product claims and approved reward events. It is the balance used by the existing withdrawal system.

How do withdrawals work?

Only eligible Winning Cash can be withdrawn. Recharge principal cannot be withdrawn. Add an active bank account, submit a request, and an administrator processes it. The current minimum is shown on the withdrawal page.

How do invitations and referrals work?

Each user has a permanent invitation code. A new user can bind one inviter before starting a purchase. Once saved, the relationship cannot be changed.

When are referral rewards credited?

After the invitee's first qualifying product purchase is successfully completed, the configured referral reward is recorded as Winning Cash for the inviter and invitee. Binding the code alone does not award money.

How can I view people I invited?

Use Invitations to see your invited users, registration details, purchase status and reward status. The relationship is loaded from the database each time the page refreshes.

How do I use My Team?

My Team shows you at the root and recursively displays users connected through permanent invitation relationships. Use the branch controls to expand or collapse larger sections.

How does the team tree work?

Each referral is a parent-to-child relationship: the inviter is the parent and the invited user is the child. The tree is built from the stored referral records, not from browser-supplied data.

How do I purchase a product?

Open Products, choose an active product, and select BUY to pay using Recharge Balance. Each product can be purchased only once per account and is then marked SOLD. The server checks the current product price and your current Recharge Balance. Winning Cash is never used for a purchase.

What are the payment and Razorpay basics?

Razorpay is used for Recharge payments. Keep the payment page open until confirmation. If a payment is interrupted, retry verification safely and do not pay again for the same recharge.

What should I do if my account has a problem?

Refresh the page and sign in again if your session expired. For a missing balance, payment, product, claim or withdrawal issue, use the support form below and include the relevant details and screenshots.
